Why Work With Me?

  • Shipped products, not just demos. Every project in my portfolio is live, real, and used by actual people — not just a GitHub repo collecting dust.
  • Full stack, end to end. I handle UI design, frontend, backend, database, and deployment — you deal with one person, not a team of strangers.
