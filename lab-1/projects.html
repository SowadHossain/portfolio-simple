Prime Fitness

Type Web Application Live URL prime-fitness.sowadh.me

What It Is

Prime Fitness is a comprehensive fitness tracking and gym management platform. Members can log workouts, track personal records, view progress over time through charts, and manage their membership status. Gym admins get a full dashboard with member analytics, attendance tracking, and revenue summaries.

Key Features

  • Workout logging with exercise library and custom routines
  • Progress analytics with charts showing strength and cardio trends over time
  • Member management dashboard for gym administrators
  • Attendance tracking and membership expiry alerts
  • Mobile-responsive interface built for use at the gym
  • Role-based access for members, trainers, and admins

Fursume — AI Pet Resume & Travel Pass Builder

Type AI-Powered Web Application Live URL dev.fursume.com

What It Is

Fursume is an AI-powered tool that helps pet owners create professional pet resumes and international travel passes for their animals. Whether you are moving abroad, applying to a pet-friendly apartment, or registering with a vet clinic in a new country, Fursume generates a clean, shareable document with all essential pet information in minutes.

Key Features

  • AI-assisted pet profile builder with smart content suggestions
  • Multiple document types: resume, travel pass, vet summary
  • Photo upload and customisable document templates
  • One-click PDF export and shareable link generation
  • Vaccination record tracking and expiry reminders
  • Support for multiple pets per account

Wardrobe — Inventory & Billing for Clothing Brands

Type Custom Management Software (Mobile-First) Live URL wardrobe.sowadh.me

What It Is

Wardrobe is a mobile-first inventory management and billing system designed specifically for clothing brands and fashion retailers. Built for use on the shop floor from a phone or tablet, it replaces the slow, error-prone spreadsheets most small fashion brands rely on. Manage your stock, record sales, generate invoices, and track revenue — all from a clean, fast mobile interface.

Key Features

  • Mobile-first design — optimised for phone and tablet use in-store
  • Product catalogue with variants (size, colour, SKU)
  • Real-time stock tracking with low-stock alerts
  • Sales recording and instant invoice generation
  • Daily, weekly, and monthly revenue summaries
  • Supplier and purchase order management
  • Offline support with background sync
