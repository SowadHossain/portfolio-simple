Common Questions

Do you work with international clients?
Yes. I work with clients worldwide. All communication is remote — email, WhatsApp, or video call. Payments via PayPal, Wise, or bank transfer.
How do payments work?
50% upfront, 50% on delivery for most projects. For larger builds we agree milestone-based payments so you pay as work is reviewed and approved.
Will I own the code?
Yes. Full ownership of all source code, assets, and deployment credentials is transferred to you upon final payment.
Do you sign NDAs?
Absolutely. Just mention it in your first message and I will send one over before we discuss anything sensitive.
What happens after delivery?
All projects include 30 days of free bug fixes post-launch. After that I offer an affordable maintenance retainer or per-hour rate for changes and new features.
Can I see more of your work?
Yes — visit sowadh.me for the full portfolio, or check github.com/SowadHossain for open-source code.
