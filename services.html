04 — SaaS & Automation

I am an automation enthusiast — I get genuinely excited about eliminating repetitive tasks. If you have a process that a human is doing more than twice, we should be automating it. I build SaaS platforms with subscription billing, multi-tenant architecture, automated workflows, and analytics dashboards that give you real insight into what is happening in your product.

What I Can Automate For You

  • Email and SMS notification pipelines
  • Report generation and scheduled data exports
  • Third-party API syncing (payment gateways, CRMs, ERPs)
  • Subscription billing with Stripe or bKash
  • Background job processing and task queues
  • Data scraping, transformation, and warehouse pipelines
